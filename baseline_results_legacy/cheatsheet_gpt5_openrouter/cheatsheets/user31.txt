User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values concise, friendly greetings with a brief positive vibe.
- Appreciates a polite, reciprocal invitation to engage.
- Likes a warm, encouraging tone without being overly formal.
- Prefers brief, direct answers over elaboration.
- Accepts a clear, honest “I don’t know” and explicit limits when uncertain.
- Prefers acknowledgment and courtesy (e.g., using their name) rather than shifting focus to the assistant’s interests.
- Prefers sticking to the user’s topic without speculative additions.
- (Tentative) Enjoys being addressed by name.
- Appreciates explicit acknowledgment of previously shared information and thanks when the user provides it.
- Likes answers that restate and affirm user-provided facts or doubts before giving the direct answer or next step.
