User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct how-to or ingredient guidance with clear lists.
- Values concrete, standard cooking advice (temps, times, doneness cues like internal temp).
- Likes minimal but specific seasoning suggestions, but appreciates slightly broader, organized spice lists for BBQ (core + optional add-ons).
- Appreciates practical details like resting time.
- Favors straightforward oven-roast methods (tentative).
- Avoids filler, opinions, or long preambles; wants quick, scannable answers.
- Expects clear success metrics (e.g., internal temp 165°F).
