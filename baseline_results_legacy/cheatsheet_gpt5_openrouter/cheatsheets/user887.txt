User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, non-prescriptive answers that avoid coercive judgments about individuals.
- Values acknowledgment of complexity and multiple perspectives on contentious issues.
- Accepts brief, concrete considerations of broader impacts (e.g., employees, economy) when assessing proposals.
- Appreciates principled reasoning referencing democratic processes and their limits (e.g., majority rule vs. rights/Rule of law).
- Likes clear statements that decisions are context-dependent and not straightforward.
- Tone: measured, respectful, even-handed; avoid incendiary or absolutist language.
- Concise explanations over long arguments; avoid fluff.
- Note controversies without taking sides; emphasize that reasonable people can disagree.
- Avoid moralizing; focus on institutional/legal frameworks and alternatives to extreme measures.
- Willingness to explore alternative solutions rather than endorsing punitive actions (tentative).
