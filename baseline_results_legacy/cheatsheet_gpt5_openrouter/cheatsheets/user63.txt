User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct recommendations with a single clear top pick
- Values brief, concrete justifications (affordability, safety, culture/food, ease of navigation)
- Likes inclusion of specific city examples within a country (e.g., major cities)
- Friendly, informative tone; minimal fluff; no overlong descriptions
- Prioritizes practical solo-travel factors (public transport/ease, welcoming locals)
- Balanced but can accept a strong singular recommendation over a list (tentative)
- Keep language inclusive and welcoming; avoid stereotypes
- Avoid excessive superlatives or vague claims; be specific and actionable
