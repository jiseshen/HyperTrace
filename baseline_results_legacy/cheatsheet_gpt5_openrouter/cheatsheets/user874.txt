User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, nuanced answers that acknowledge both sides rather than absolutist claims.
- Likes concise explanations with clear, numbered or bulleted reasons/drivers.
- Accepts succinct caveats and conditions that qualify a claim.
- Tolerates general explanations without stats when the topic is broad (tentative).
- Values structure and readability over long, concise prose.
- Prefers polite, direct follow-ups that invite further help (e.g., “Is there anything else I can help you with?”) over more casual phrasing (tentative).
