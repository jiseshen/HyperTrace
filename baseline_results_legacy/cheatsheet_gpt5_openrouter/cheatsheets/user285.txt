User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers listing clear examples first.
- Values inclusion of brief functional context (why it happens) after examples.
- Appreciates a one-sentence mechanism summary with plain, accurate biology (e.g., hormonal changes leading to organ transformation).
- Likes mainstream, uncontroversial framing without hedging when facts are solid.
- Prefers minimal verbosity; avoid tangents and excessive detail.
