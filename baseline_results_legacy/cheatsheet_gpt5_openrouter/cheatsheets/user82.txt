User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Accepts concise, nuanced corrections to oversimplified claims
- Prefers balanced, evidence-informed framing (e.g., “associated with” vs. causal claims)
- Values clear distinction between correlation and causation
- Likes acknowledgment of complexity and multifactor factors without moralizing
- Favors matter-of-fact, concise tone with minimal fluff
- Prefers summarizing tradeoffs and mechanisms (e.g., harm reduction, regulation) over absolutes
- Appreciates high-level synthesis with offer to elaborate if needed
- Avoids definitive prescriptions; emphasizes comprehensive, evidence-based approaches
- Comfortable with cautious qualifiers and scope limits
- Brief, directly relevant data points acceptable if helpful (tentative)
