User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, neutral explanations with clear mention of uncertainty without excessive hedging.
- Likes brief lists of concrete drivers/factors shaping outcomes (e.g., key variables influencing interest rates).
- Accepts high-level, non-committal framing about future trajectories without specific forecasts.
- Tolerates general scenario framing when precise predictions aren’t possible.
- Appreciates an optional offer to provide more detail on specific variables or scenarios after a self-contained summary.
- Dislikes overly formal or legalistic disclaimers; keep caveats short and relevant.
- (Tentative) Comfortable with global framing when specifics (e.g., country/central bank) are not provided.
