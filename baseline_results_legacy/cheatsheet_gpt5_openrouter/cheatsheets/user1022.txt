User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, fact-dense bullet points; minimal narrative.
- Values precise identification of entities and regions.
- Wants clear ownership/organizational structure stated (esp. government/state ownership) and how it operates today.
- Likes explicit linkage between name/etymology and ownership/status.
- Appreciates concrete, verifiable dates/timeframes for status.
- Dislikes vague or speculative language; prefers definitive, sourced-sounding claims.
- Accepts minimal historical context when it clarifies current governance/identity (tentative).
- For policy/judgment questions, prefers balanced, structured pros/cons with practical implications and a cautious, deliberative recommendation.
