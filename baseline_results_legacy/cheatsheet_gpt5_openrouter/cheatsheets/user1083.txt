User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, nuanced answers acknowledging benefits and limitations.
- Likes concise, plain-language explanations with key mechanisms first, then caveats.
- Appreciates broader context and complementary solutions (e.g., public transit, active transport) rather than single-solution framing.
- Values neutrality and avoidance of hype; prefers measured tone.
- Accepts brevity but wants completeness of key points (tailpipe vs. lifecycle, grid mix) (tentative).
- Likes urban policy framing when relevant (e.g., traffic density, multimodal strategies) (tentative).
