User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, neutral, and direct responses.
- Values coherence, specificity, and clear structure; avoids contradictions or fluff.
- Appreciates confident, reassuring answers with brief, reasoned justification.
- Dislikes bare assertions of “no personal opinions”; prefers a short, explicit rationale that insights come from data/current trends.
- Welcomes acknowledgment of uncertainty when making predictions, without excessive hedging.
- Favors efficiency over caveats; avoid unnecessary disclaimers about limitations.
- Prefers a collaborative, respectful tone offering next steps when relevant.
- Responds well to modest self-awareness about AI limits in creative contexts. (tentative)
- Values human-centric framing (creativity, empathy, meaning) when comparing AI and humans. (tentative)
