User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Nuanced, empathetic, non-judgmental tone; normalize fear and stigma around help-seeking.
- Frame depression as a medical condition; emphasize that seeking help is strength, not weakness.
- Provide clear, step-by-step, actionable guidance for initiating help (e.g., start small, scripts, what to expect).
- Encourage self-compassion and acknowledge recovery as a process with normal setbacks.
- Offer practical preparation tips to reduce anxiety (e.g., questions to ask in an initial consult).
- Keep advice concise and concrete over vague reassurance.
- Include safety/support options when relevant (tentative).
