User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with a clear stance and brief reasoning.
- Values practical, balanced suggestions (structure + flexibility) over platitudes.
- Likes concrete, relatable examples (e.g., family meals, game nights, outdoor time, volunteering).
- Appreciates emphasis on open communication and balance between fun and responsibility.
- Neutral, non-hyped tone.
- Dislikes overlong, prescriptive advice or moralizing.
- Avoids involving others unnecessarily (e.g., experts, community) unless essential.
- Accepts brief, solution-oriented qualifiers after the main stance (tentative).
