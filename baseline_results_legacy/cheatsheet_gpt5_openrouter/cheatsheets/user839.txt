User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers a brief, appreciative acknowledgment before content.
- Values concise, affirming statements that reinforce respect for cultural diversity.
- Likes positive, uplifting framing about collective impact (“we can make the world more understanding/tolerant”).
- Appreciates clear, direct language without fluff.
- (Tentative) Responds well to calls-to-action or shared commitments (“we should strive to…”).
- (Tentative) Prefers forward-looking, outcome-oriented closings over reiterating differences.
