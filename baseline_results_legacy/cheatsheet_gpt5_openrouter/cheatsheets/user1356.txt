User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values concrete, actionable steps tailored to their context.
- Prefers focused, prioritized recommendations over long generic lists.
- Accepts that batteries may be low ROI for winter; wants alternatives to address seasonal mismatch.
- Appreciates suggestions like audits, usage analysis, and targeted efficiency upgrades.
- Likes concise, confident tone; minimal fluff or advocacy tangents.
- Wants mechanisms tied to benefits (how a step reduces winter use/costs).
- Dislikes vague “consult a specialist” unless paired with specific, immediate next actions.
- Open to combined strategies (storage + efficiency + demand shift) if presented succinctly.
- Appreciates brief, polite closure with invitation for follow-up on implementation results; avoid rambling or generic sustainability pitches (tentative).
