User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers direct, concise answers that avoid sensationalism.
- Values balanced, evidence-based framing over listing conspiracy narratives.
- Dislikes speculative or detailed recitation of conspiracy theories.
- Accepts nuanced uncertainty when warranted (e.g., acknowledging limits of evidence) without excessive hedging.
- Appreciates focus on real, observable influences (e.g., powerful individuals/institutions) rather than secretive claims.
- (Tentative) Prefers generalizable, sober summaries over name-dropping specific controversial groups.
- Prefers informative, non-judgmental responses over blanket refusals when discussing sensitive topics (within safety bounds).
