User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, empathetic acknowledgment of distress/escalation.
- Values clear, direct guidance to disengage from harmful or no-win interactions.
- Appreciates prioritizing personal well-being over continuing unproductive debates.
- Likes actionable, low-friction strategies (e.g., set boundaries, limit contact, take a break).
- Prefers nonjudgmental, supportive tone without pressure to keep engaging.
- Responds well to firm, unambiguous recommendations when boundaries are crossed.
- Appreciates suggestions to involve a neutral third party/trusted adult to mediate family conflicts (tentative).
- Values concrete boundary-setting advice with consequences (e.g., limit or distance if violated).
