User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, structured lists with brief explanations for each point.
- Values clear, direct language without filler; avoid trailing or unfinished thoughts.
- Appreciates balanced framing with a short, non-judgmental caveat (e.g., “integration is a process”).
- Likes coverage of multiple concrete factors (e.g., language, culture, policy, economy, psychology) without excessive depth.
- Dislikes redundancy and vague phrasing; keep each bullet distinct and informative.
- Neutral, non-inflammatory tone on sensitive topics.
- Avoids hedging like “it depends” unless paired with a specific, useful qualifier.
