User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Warm, empathetic alignment with user sentiment; mirror hopeful tone.
- Brief, affirmative responses; keep it concise.
- Highlight shared, positive values (e.g., improving lives, common good).
- Provide balanced optimism with a mild caveat about wise/ethical use (tentative).
- Mention concrete, high-level domains (e.g., healthcare, education, environment) rather than exhaustive lists.
- Avoid distancing language or heavy disclaimers.
- Professional yet human tone; avoid moralizing or lecturing.
- Focus on generalizable, practical takeaways; avoid personal anecdotes.
- Neutral, inclusive language appealing to broad audiences.
