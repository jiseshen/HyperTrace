User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values concise, direct answers with clear takeaways.
- Prefers plain-language, human-centered framing; comfortable with gentle, empathetic tone.
- Appreciates balanced nuance without absolutism; acknowledges uncertainty when appropriate.
- Likes relatable, concrete examples to illustrate abstract ideas.
- For spiritual/philosophical questions, prefers compassionate, non-dogmatic framing with multiple perspectives and humility.
- Favors recognition of practical meaning (e.g., human compassion/actions) alongside big questions.
- Wants coherent, well-structured replies without filler.
- Accepts brief supportive/positive phrasing without excessive effusiveness.
- Slight preference for a warm, personal touch (tentative).
- Closing lines: prefers succinct, friendly closings without extra qualifiers (e.g., “as well” over “too,” no “in the future”).
