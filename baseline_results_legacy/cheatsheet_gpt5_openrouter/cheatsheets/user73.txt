User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values concise, clear synthesis without moralizing.
- Prefers balanced, nonjudgmental framing that acknowledges multiple perspectives; avoids absolutes or hierarchy.
- Appreciates emphasis on recognizing positive actions over scrutinizing motives; avoid motivation-policing.
- Wants practical, solution-oriented guidance that encourages supportive, prosocial norms.
- Favors empathetic, even-handed language that validates concerns while promoting harm reduction.
- Responds well to framing that reduces conflict by focusing on mutual understanding and shared goals.
- Prefers acknowledging subjectivity and context-dependence over one-size-fits-all answers.
- Likes clear, concrete examples that contrast scenarios to illustrate a point.
- Responds well to explicit affirmation of personal preference subjectivity (e.g., “there’s no right or wrong; it’s up to you”).
- Prefers constructive, respectful criticism that centers impact and context over assigning blame or focusing on intent.
