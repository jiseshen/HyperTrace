User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, professional, empathetic tone.
- Values neutrality and non-judgmental framing; avoids hard moral stances.
- Accepts brief acknowledgment of AI limits, then expects substantive guidance.
- Appreciates multiple perspectives and individual differences.
- Wants practical, actionable guidance with attention to emotional impacts.
- Open to optional suggestions for professional help/resources.
- Responds well to autonomy-respecting language; no shaming or pressure.
- Prefers clear, scannable takeaways; minimal fluff.
- Likes concrete, credible resource suggestions (named orgs/sites) over vague offers; welcomes a brief list with an invitation to tailor specifics.
- (tentative) Appreciates brief, warm closure with an open invitation to return (e.g., “don’t hesitate to reach out”).
