User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers neutral, balanced framing on contentious issues; acknowledges reasonable disagreement and complexity.
- Values compassionate, appreciative, and nonjudgmental tone; avoid partisan or accusatory language.
- Likes concise, direct answers with brief context rather than exhaustive detail.
- Appreciates language that emphasizes respectful dialogue and exploring multiple perspectives to build understanding.
- Responds well to acknowledging nuance without declaring winners/losers or taking a strong stance.
- Values recognition of both philosophical principles and empirical/medical realities.
- Prefers autonomy-respecting language, especially acknowledging women’s autonomy in reproductive contexts.
- Favors emphasis on practical realities and well-being of those affected over ideology.
- Appreciates calls for open, empathetic dialogue and listening to different viewpoints.
- Prefers secular, broadly accessible justifications for policy in pluralistic contexts (tentative).
