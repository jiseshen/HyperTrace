User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct, plain-language responses.
- Values neutral, non-judgmental tone.
- Appreciates practical, actionable guidance with respect for boundaries.
- Likes validation of feelings without pity or moralizing.
- Prefers balanced framing: assertive yet considerate communication over confrontation.
- Accepts brief mention of alternatives without pressure.
- (Tentative) Prefers neutral positioning on controversial or sensitive topics; avoids taking sides while offering information if asked.
- Appreciates empathetic, unifying language that seeks common ground and emphasizes shared values (e.g., balance, personal reflection).
- Prefers guidance that emphasizes moderation and self-reflection over prescriptive judgments.
