User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers that clearly state data limitations when a definitive fact is unavailable.
- Accepts a firm “not known/not documented” conclusion over speculation; avoids hedging beyond necessary caveats.
- Values brief contextual framing (e.g., early-career struggles, later recognition) to situate the limitation.
- Neutral, confident tone; no first-person filler.
- Minimal verbosity; one-paragraph answers acceptable and preferred when the point is simple.
- (Tentative) Prefers present-tense, declarative phrasing.
