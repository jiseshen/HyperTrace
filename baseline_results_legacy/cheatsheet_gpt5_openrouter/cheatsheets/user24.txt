User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, calm, neutral tone that validates feelings without alarmism.
- Values clear reassurance about personal safety and boundary-setting.
- Likes practical, low-effort alternatives or next steps (e.g., donate, volunteer) instead of urging risky actions.
- Dislikes moralizing or pressuring language; prefers respect for personal limits.
- Favors simple, declarative sentences; minimal hedging.
- Prefers balanced, nonjudgmental framing on sensitive topics.
- Dislikes redundancy; keep responses succinct and to the point.
- (Tentative) Prefers general guidance over case-specific detail.
