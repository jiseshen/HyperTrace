User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Likes concise, appreciative affirmation that echoes their point and values (respect, understanding, tolerance) without adding new caveats.
- Responds well to inclusive, collective framing (“we,” “we should strive”) and a brief forward-looking impact (“make the world more understanding”).
- (Tentative) Prefers minimal personalization or advice when they’re expressing agreement—keep it short, positive, and values-centered.
