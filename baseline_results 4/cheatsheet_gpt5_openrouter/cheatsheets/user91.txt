User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, neutral framing on sensitive political/economic questions.
- Accepts uncertainty language but wants concrete factors and trade-offs.
- Likes structured, scenario-based comparisons with clear headings and bullet points.
- Values completeness over brevity when outlining considerations, even for “short” prompts (tentative).
- Prefers clear, direct sentences without heavy caveats or hedging.
- Headline-style opener followed by numbered or bulleted points works well (tentative).
- Includes country-specific context while noting broader, general factors.
- Tolerates partial/incomplete coverage if the structure and comparative scenarios are clear (tentative).
