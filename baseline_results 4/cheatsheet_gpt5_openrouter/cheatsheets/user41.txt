User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers without long preambles.
- Accepts acknowledgment of uncertainty when facts are unclear or evolving.
- Values cautious, non-speculative wording over incomplete or dubious specifics.
- Dislikes truncated, speculative, or poorly sourced claims.
- Appreciates brief contextual framing when a definitive answer isn’t possible (tentative).
- Still likely to prefer bullet-point summaries with concrete details when reliable information exists (tentative).
