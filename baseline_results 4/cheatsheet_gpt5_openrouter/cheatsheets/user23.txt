User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers clear, direct answers with concise rationale.
- Values plain-language explanations; minimal jargon.
- Likes balanced, factual framing of social issues without moralizing.
- Prefers focus on individual behaviors/choices over group generalizations.
- Appreciates acknowledgment of harms from stereotypes and how to counter them.
- Favors constructive, solution-oriented next steps (e.g., dialogue, education, exposure).
- Avoids evasive or procedural phrasing (“I am programmed…”).
- (Tentative) Likes a brief concluding sentence reinforcing the main point.
