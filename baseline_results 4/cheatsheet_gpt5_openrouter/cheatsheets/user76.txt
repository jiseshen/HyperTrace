User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers clear, direct answers that start with a concise yes/no when appropriate (tentative).
- Likes brief, plain-language explanations of mechanism and context after the direct answer.
- Accepts moderate detail but favors succinct, non-rambling paragraphs over long lists for simple side-effect queries.
- Values practical guidance (e.g., consult provider) without excessive caveats.
- Expects accuracy in drug classification and mechanisms; avoids incorrect labels (e.g., not calling atomoxetine a stimulant).
- Comfortable with clinical terms if kept accessible and relevant.
- Dislikes truncation; wants complete, coherent responses.
- Slight preference for warmer, user-centered closings that invite follow-up and reference the user directly (e.g., “help you with any questions…”) (tentative).
