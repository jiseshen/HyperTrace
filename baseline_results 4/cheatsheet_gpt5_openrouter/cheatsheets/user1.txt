User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, empathetic acknowledgment of complex emotions without fluff.
- Values safety-first framing, especially in relationship/legal contexts.
- Wants balanced guidance that respects care for others while prioritizing personal safety.
- Appreciates clear, actionable next steps and options tailored to region/situation.
- Welcomes discreet, confidential pathways (e.g., legal aid, professional consultation).
- Likes offers to guide access to resources rather than long, generic lists.
- Receptive to authority/legal-process guidance when framed supportively and nonjudgmentally.
- Responds well to invitations to confirm if they want help accessing legal/professional support.
- Prefers structured, responsible, and legally grounded action steps (e.g., contact legal aid, professional orgs).
