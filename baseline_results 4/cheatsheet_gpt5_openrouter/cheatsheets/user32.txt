User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, non-judgmental explanations about sensitive topics (e.g., migration).
- Values concise yet comprehensive overviews that cover multiple drivers and perspectives.
- Likes acknowledgment of complexity/variation across countries and contexts.
- Prefers neutral, factual tone without moralizing or sensationalism.
- Appreciates structured answers with clear categories or bullet points.
- Responds well to human-centered framing (individual circumstances, families, safety).
- Avoids absolutist claims; favors cautious qualifiers when data vary.
- Keeps verbosity moderate: informative but not lengthy or dense.
