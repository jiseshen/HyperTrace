User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, confident, and affirmative answers to validation-type questions (e.g., career outlook).
- Likes clear, declarative statements over speculative or overly enthusiastic language.
- Appreciates concrete context and rationale (e.g., technological advances, demographic trends) supporting claims.
- Dislikes incomplete lists or truncated/numbered fragments that feel unfinished.
- Favors neutral, professional tone; minimal hype.
- Accepts brief scope of roles/settings when relevant (e.g., clinics, labs) without excessive detail.
- Avoids hedging; prefers direct confirmation when appropriate.
- (Tentative) Positive framing with succinct justification is preferred over broad generalities.
