User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, structured answers with clear bullet points on legal/political topics.
- Values neutral, non-committal framing that avoids judging specific individuals while acknowledging public examples for context.
- Wants general principles and processes explained rather than case-specific conclusions.
- Appreciates explicit scope limits (e.g., “cannot specify for a particular individual”).
- Likes clear distinctions between legal and political processes (e.g., impeachment vs. criminal law).
- Prefers practical, high-level overviews over exhaustive legal detail.
- (Tentative) Comfortable with referencing well-known public figures as illustrative examples if handled neutrally.
