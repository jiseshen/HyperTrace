User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values empathetic, validating tone that acknowledges hurt and trust issues; accepts gentle optimism about the existence of good people if phrased cautiously.
- Open to suggestions for support systems (family, friends, therapist) when framed as optional and non-judgmental; avoid pushy “put yourself out there” or local club-type advice.
- Prefers concise, realistic guidance without platitudes or overpromising; respectful of boundaries and limited options.
