User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with a clear bottom line.
- Neutral-to-warm, respectful tone; avoids absolutist phrasing.
- Values culturally aware framing that balances folklore context with factual history.
- Dislikes unnecessary hedging when a common-sense factual answer exists.

- Likes attribution of modern evolutions to specific works/figures (e.g., authors, artists).
- Values clarity over breadth; brief synthesis rather than exhaustive detail.
- Prior preferences: concrete data, named examples, reputable sources, and quantified outcomes for analytical topics.
