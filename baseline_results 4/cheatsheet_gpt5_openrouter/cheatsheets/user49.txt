User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, complete no-bake dessert recipes with ingredients grouped by component (base, filling, topping).
- Wants clear, step-by-step instructions without fluff.
- Expects UK-friendly terms and measures (digestive biscuits, double cream, caster sugar; grams/ml).
- Prefers full-fat, high-quality dairy (full-fat cream cheese, double cream).
- Values practical, accessible ingredients like canned caramel/dulce de leche.
- Likes explicit quantities and specific items (ripe bananas count, caramel condensed milk).
- Appreciates a clear topping section with vanilla-sweetened whipped cream and simple decorations (banana slices, caramel drizzle).
- Prefers ingredients and instructions provided together in a single, self-contained response.
- Likes festive/seasonal decoration ideas that are edible, simple, and classic (gingerbread crumbs, cinnamon, chocolate shavings, winter berries/citrus).
