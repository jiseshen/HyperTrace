User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, structured, bullet-point or list-style answers.
- Values concrete, actionable suggestions over abstract discussion.
- Likes coverage at both individual and system/policy levels for the same topic.
- Appreciates brief cause-effect explanations linking actions to outcomes.
- Neutral, straightforward tone without fluff.
- Dislikes vagueness, truncation, or incomplete thoughts.
- (Tentative) Favors pragmatic, balanced recommendations over ideological takes.
- (Tentative) Likes inclusion of diversified strategies with practical examples.
