User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers empathetic, non-judgmental guidance that emphasizes understanding the other person first.
- Likes actionable, concrete tips presented as concise bullet points.
- Values wording that reduces defensiveness (e.g., “ask open-ended questions,” “find common ground,” “acknowledge feelings”).
- Responds well to gentle, patient framing (e.g., “over time,” “leave doors open,” “good faith efforts”).
- Appreciates focusing on respect and not attacking; goal framed as helping people feel heard.
- Favors balanced tone that encourages dialogue without pressuring immediate agreement or conversion.
- Dislikes platitudes; prefers practical steps with brief rationale.
- (Tentative) Positive about compassionate language that hints at potential growth without sounding preachy.
