User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers clear, direct definitions in full, non-truncated sentences.
- Likes concise, plain-language phrasing without jargon.
- Values comprehensive but succinct coverage that spans beliefs, behaviors, and institutions.
- Favors neutral, non-accusatory tone on sensitive topics.
- Appreciates acknowledgment of multiple levels (individual to systemic) in brief form.
- Prefers scannable, compact responses over long prose.
- (Tentative) Responds well to balanced framing that notes inequality/oppression without moralizing.
- Dislikes incomplete or cut-off answers; wants fully formed, polished text.
