User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, neutral, fact-based analysis over emotive or moralizing language.
- Values rights-based, democratic principles as the framework for evaluating trade-offs.
- Likes clear, firm conclusions that reject false dilemmas (e.g., survival vs morality need not be binary).
- Appreciates arguments that show survival and ethical commitments can be jointly pursued.
- Responds well to warnings against “ends justify the means” erosion of rights, stated briefly.
- Favors focused summaries with a few concrete, principled reasons over exhaustive debates.
- Prefers descriptive analysis with justified normative stance; avoids dismissive labels.
- Likes comparisons that highlight better alternatives that protect rights while achieving security.
- (Tentative) Accepts survival as paramount while emphasizing that moral commitments give survival its value.
