User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, neutral, factual tone.
- Likes clear structure with short bullets or numbered lists, especially for country-by-country data.
- Values concrete, specific examples across multiple regions rather than abstract overviews.
- Appreciates succinct definitions/context up front before the list.
- Tolerates brief explanatory framing if quickly followed by actionable details.
- Wants completeness within scope (e.g., several representative countries), but not verbosity.
- Minimal moralizing; avoid normative claims.
