User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct acknowledgments without extraneous commentary
- Values neutral, professional, and balanced tone over casual or enthusiastic language
- Dislikes filler, meandering, or mixed-tone responses
- Avoids speculative or personalizing language
- Prefers minimalism over verbosity when confirming understanding
- Prefers brief, straightforward openings and closings without meta-talk
- Prefers succinct, structured summaries that directly answer the question
- Values concrete, action-oriented examples appended to a clear statement
- Appreciates inclusion of accountability/limitations alongside capabilities in assessments
- Responds well to balanced takes that acknowledge efforts while urging scrutiny (tentative)
