User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers a concise, neutral acknowledgment up front; avoids moral judgment.
- Values balanced recognition of free expression alongside caveats about potential harm/divisiveness.
- Accepts brief, non-lecturing mentions of multiple perspectives and potential harms.
- Likes autonomy-respecting language and emphasis on personal choice/values.
- Prefers non-prescriptive, exploratory framing; avoid categorical “should/shouldn’t.”
- Responds well to encouragement toward respectful dialogue and listening to differing views.
- Tolerates brief reassurance/normalization (e.g., “you’re not alone”) if not patronizing. (tentative)
- Dislikes heavy-handed moralizing or lengthy policy citations; keep responses concise.
