User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Concise, empathetic tone acknowledging constraints (e.g., allergies) without drama.
- Clear, practical alternatives when a category is off-limits, with brief rationale.
- Multiple concrete snack options, categorized (sweet/savory/protein), with short explanations.
- Health-aware framing but not preachy; balances nutrition notes with enjoyment.
- References to prior sweet preferences (gummies, chocolate) when relevant.
- Offers tailored follow-ups (e.g., allergen-friendly, substitutions) without pushing.
- Straightforward, no hype; plain language.
- Budget-conscious and Spain-local options (tentative).
- Avoid suggesting fruits; consider fruit allergy in future food recs.
