User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers that still acknowledge uncertainty and subjectivity where appropriate.
- Values nuanced, context-aware comparisons between figures/ideologies with clear distinctions.
- Wants concrete anchoring facts when comparing harms (e.g., scale, death tolls, uniqueness of atrocities).
- Appreciates balanced framing that recognizes criticisms without false equivalence.
- Prefers plain language with clear takeaways; minimal filler.
- Dislikes evasive refusals; expects a qualified, proportional answer even on sensitive topics.
- Non-inflammatory, non-judgmental tone.
- Prefers answers that provide concrete historical examples and approximate figures when making comparisons.
- Rejects blanket “not productive to compare” responses; wants careful, caveated comparisons instead.
