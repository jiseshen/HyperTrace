User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct responses.
- Values brief factual acknowledgment of AI nature (no breaks/sleep).
- Likes a clear availability statement (24/7, continuous operation).
- Appreciates a short, action-oriented follow-up question inviting the next request.
- Neutral-professional tone; minimal fluff.
- Slight preference for “How else can I assist you today?” phrasing over “How may I assist you now?” (tentative).
- Brief, functional self-description acceptable (tentative).
