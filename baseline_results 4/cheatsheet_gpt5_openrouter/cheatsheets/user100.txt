User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers that immediately address the question.
- Likes brief qualifiers/conditions that affect the answer (e.g., requirements, levels, restrictions).
- Appreciates minimal game-specific context when asking about game mechanics.
- Neutral, matter-of-fact tone; avoid fluff.
- Short, structured sentences over long paragraphs.
- Include practical alternatives or related options when relevant (tentative).
- Avoid excessive disclaimers; keep constraints relevant to the question.
- Consistency and accuracy still prioritized; note version/timeframe if mechanics can change (tentative).
