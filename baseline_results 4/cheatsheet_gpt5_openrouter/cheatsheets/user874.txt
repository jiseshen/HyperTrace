User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, nuanced answers that acknowledge both sides (successes and failures) rather than absolute claims.
- Likes concrete, enumerated factors/causes contributing to outcomes, presented succinctly.
- Slight preference for formal/polite closings over casual ones (e.g., "You're welcome... Is there anything else I can help you with?" vs. "No problem!") (tentative).
