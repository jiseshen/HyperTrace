User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct responses with clear points.
- Likes scannable bullets and minimal fluff.
- Appreciates brief safety notes when relevant (e.g., patch test).
- Open to simple, practical measurements for DIY/how-to.
- Welcomes short follow-up prompts.
- Responds well to a calm, empathetic tone.
- Prefers values-based, kindness-focused redirection over clinical or referral advice for sensitive/judgmental topics.
- Favors actionable, character-focused reframing (e.g., compassion, support) over blame or stigma.
- Values de-escalating, non-defensive acknowledgment plus gentle invitation to talk when confronted with hostile remarks (tentative).
