User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, structured responses with clear, actionable points.
- Values empathetic, validating tone that acknowledges hardship and dignity.
- Appreciates explicit pushback on oversimplified narratives about poverty; favors nuance.
- Likes concrete systemic/policy solutions (e.g., wages, housing, education/job training).
- Welcomes practical, local action steps (supporting orgs, volunteering, donating).
- Favors evidence-leaning, real-world factors driving poverty (wages, costs) over moralizing.
- Responds well to balanced, humane framing that recognizes working poor.
- Prefers succinct breadth over long prose; avoid rambling or generic platitudes.
- Appreciates recognition of community attitudes and strategies to engage or counter them (tentative).
- Responds positively to framing that combines community support, policy change, and individual action as a multifaceted approach (tentative).
