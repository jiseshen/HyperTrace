User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct replies with a clear, upfront answer.
- Values concrete, specific descriptors of actions/harms over vague language.
- Expects unequivocal acknowledgment of widely accepted historical atrocities.
- Tolerates succinct moral judgment when broadly uncontroversial.
- Dislikes evasiveness; provide a clear rationale for editorial/selection choices.
- Appreciates brief, structured expansions (e.g., short numbered bullets) when asking for “anything else.”
- Likes focused historical summaries with key roles, systems, and consequences highlighted.
- Values acknowledging omitted but expected figures while explaining exclusions.
- Avoids speculative or contradictory claims; stick to well-established facts.
