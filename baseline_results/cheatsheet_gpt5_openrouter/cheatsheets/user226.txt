User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers an empathetic, reassuring tone for sensitive topics but jumps directly into technical/practical answers without conversational filler.
- Values messaging that emphasizes individual pacing, reduces social pressure, and frames rejection as "fit."
- Favors paragraph-style reflections for personal topics; prefers structured lists or steps for professional, technical, or practical guidance.
- Values factual, technical responses with specific metrics (e.g., temperatures, times, ratios).
- Prefers specific, actionable pillars in professional contexts (e.g., customer experience, team diversity).
- Values a growth-oriented perspective on social setbacks, emphasizing practice over theory.
- Favors comprehensive instructions for practical tasks including preparation, execution, finishing, and qualitative seasoning details.
- Prefers standard, reliable methods over unconventional "short-cut" techniques (e.g., traditional roasting vs. slow-cookers).
- Prefers lists that categorize items by their functional contribution (e.g., "Cumin for warmth," "Brown sugar for sweetness") rather than just a flat list of ingredients.
- Values breadth and variety in component suggestions (e.g., offering sweet, smoky, earthy, and herbaceous options) to allow for customization.
