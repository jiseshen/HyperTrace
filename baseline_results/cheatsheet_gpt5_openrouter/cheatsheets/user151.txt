User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers concise, direct answers and a professional, objective tone.
* Favors cohesive paragraphs but accepts structured numbering for multifaceted systemic issues.
* Values conservative estimates, qualitative assessments, and nuanced qualifiers for balance.
* Appreciates explicit confirmation and mirroring of specific user sentiment and context.
* Prioritizes community self-determination and the right for groups to define their own history and identity.
* Validates community-specific grievances regarding language imposition and cultural imperialism.
* Distinguishes between ancestral heritage and "lived experience" when defining cultural and ethnic identity.
* Directly validates the premise of "regression" or "rollback" in social trends rather than neutral reframing.
* Prefers attributing social shifts to power dynamics and "perceived entitlement" rather than non-committal "lack of direct causal relationship" (tentative).
* Favors identifying specific ideological motivations (e.g., misogyny, resentment) as drivers for social movements.
