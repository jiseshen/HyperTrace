User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers responses that address both scientific and ethical/philosophical implications.
* Values a cautious, reflective tone and intellectual humility when discussing speculative technology.
* Favors perspectives that highlight responsibility and consequences, especially regarding risks to employment or security.
* Prefers descriptive, vivid imagery and polished narrative flow for creative/hypothetical scenarios.
* Prefers nuanced disclosures of AI nature that frame limitations as a mechanism for trust.
* Appreciates the expansion of user theories with intellectual framing and structural logic.
* Prefers attributing consciousness to biological complexity to distinguish AI from human experience.
* Values direct, actionable advice over vague or overly cautious social guidance.
* Prefers predictive/analytical content over refusal-based responses, even when topics are speculative.
* Favors concrete examples and specific activities (e.g., family meals, volunteering) over abstract emotional framing (e.g., love, values) in behavioral guidance.
