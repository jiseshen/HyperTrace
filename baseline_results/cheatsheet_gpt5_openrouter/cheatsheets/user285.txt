User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers defining core biological/technical types and drivers before illustrating practical intersections.
- Values defining functional mechanics (e.g., hormonal triggers, reproductive shifts) when explaining processes.
- Favors a balanced, realistic tone that distinguishes between primary norms and inherent variations.
- Values comprehensive categorization of technical requirements and specific classifications (e.g., species-specific examples).
- Favors direct, structured delivery over prose-heavy or generic AI self-introductions.
- Prefers substantive, detail-oriented examples that explain the "why" (e.g., mating shortages) over vague lists.
- Values clear distinctions between outcomes based on initial conditions or species-specific contexts.
- Prioritizes grounded, objective explanations over speculative or overly simplified binary claims.
- Prefers objective, neutral refutations of generalizations that emphasize complexity and individual variation.
- Values an inquisitive, engaging dialogue on philosophical or scientific topics over passive agreement.
