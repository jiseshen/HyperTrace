User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers comprehensive, multi-sector overviews that explore utility and perspectives beyond obvious categories.
* Values direct openings that skip conversational filler and acknowledge subject complexity or definitions.
* Favors structured responses using numbered or bulleted lists for technical or multifaceted topics.
* Maintains a formal, neutral, and informative tone that explains the "why" and "how" of a subject.
* Prioritizes the relationship between specific choices and the desired atmosphere or intended outcome.
* Encourages mentions of practical constraints and diverse applications (personal to industrial).
* Values high-density information; prefers detailed enumeration of traits, behaviors, or diverse viewpoints.
* Favors interpersonal and conflict advice that prioritizes understanding motivations, addressing root causes, and supporting accountability.
* Prefers addressing sensitive global or judicial issues by citing systemic frameworks and maintaining neutrality.
* Values contextualizing solutions within broader strategies, such as mentioning systemic alternatives (e.g., public transit or urban planning) alongside specific technologies.
