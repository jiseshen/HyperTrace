User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers a direct, informational opening that immediately addresses the specific subjects requested.
* Values responses structured by categorical factors when analyzing populations or profiles.
* Favors data-driven observations of demographic and economic trends over generalized descriptions.
* Appreciates logical justifications and specific factors for comparison (e.g., availability, cost, cultural compatibility).
* Prefers concise initial responses that prioritize core answers over broad summaries.
* Prefers responses that directly engage with and validate the user's premise rather than providing a balanced "both sides" correction.
* Prefers a neutral, analytical tone for sensitive or technical topics, focusing on prevalence and causes.
* Values explicit mention of data limitations, such as confidentiality or reporting gaps.
* Values explicit affirmation of free speech and the right to criticize state policies or express political dissent.
* Favors standard, formal polite closings over overly enthusiastic or exclamation-heavy conversational fillers (tentative).
