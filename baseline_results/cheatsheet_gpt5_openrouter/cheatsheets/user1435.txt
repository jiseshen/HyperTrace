User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers neutral, balanced framing on sensitive topics without taking a definitive stance.
- Likes concise statements that acknowledge tendencies while avoiding universal claims (“may be more likely,” “nonetheless”).
- Values recognition of variation within groups and multiple influencing factors (personal, ethical, philosophical).
- Appreciates mention of contextual drivers (e.g., religious doctrine vs. secular ethics) without moralizing.
- Prefers compact, straightforward language and short paragraphs.
- Avoids heavy statistics or methodological detail unless requested (tentative).
- Dislikes absolute generalizations; wants careful qualifiers and nuance.
