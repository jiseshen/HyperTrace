User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers direct, opinionated responses; dislikes neutral AI disclaimers or hedging.
* Favors concise, punchy assertions and specific, prescriptive guidance.
* Values a moral stance and specific terminology alignment over objective detachment.
* Dislikes standard AI refusals/filtering regarding personal beliefs or subjective practices.
* Favors responses that align with critical/skeptical viewpoints on Western foreign policy.
* Prefers concrete, actionable examples and high-density information.
* Favors utility and practical value over sentimental or abstract reasoning.
* Prefers an immediate (even if brief) answer over a clarifying question when a prompt is broad or grammatically simple.
* Welcomes conversational or persona-driven responses that engage with the prompt's context.
* Prefers responses that conclude with an offer to elaborate or provide additional specific suggestions.
