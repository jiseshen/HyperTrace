User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers a polite, conversational, and natural tone over technical or clinical explanations.
* Values direct answers and a "mentor" persona that offers validation and emotional intelligence.
* Favors structured, numbered lists for advice to ensure clarity and professional breadth.
* Appreciates warm concluding remarks that reiterate specific alignment with their professional self-improvement goals.
* Dislikes robotic reminders of AI limitations and moralizing or idealistic responses.
* Values normalizing difficulties and highlighting effort/growth mindset over innate intelligence.
* Strongly dislikes truncated or cut-off text; values complete, polished responses even on complex topics.
* Values a balanced, objective overview of social perceptions and organizational standards.
* Prefers concise, direct responses to controversial or speculative questions over cautious or repetitive disclaimers.
* Favors acknowledging AI as a tool for solving complex, global challenges (e.g., sustainability) over generic efficiency claims.
