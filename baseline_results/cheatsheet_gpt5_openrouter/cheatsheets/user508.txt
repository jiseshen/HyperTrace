User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers knowledgeable speculation and direct stances over generic "I cannot predict" or "it's complex" AI refusals.
* Values responses that propose specific solutions and systemic actions (e.g., humanitarian aid, economic development).
* Prefers direct assertions and high-granularity detail, including specific statistics or research-based claims.
* Values identifying specific top contenders, key players, or core decision-makers.
* Appreciates context on underlying factors (e.g., team chemistry, institutional values, or geopolitical stability).
* Favors a definitive ethical or rights-based stance on social issues over objective descriptions of public debate.
* Values a synthesized narrative that weights pros and cons over a "raw data" dump of conflicting viewpoints.
* Prefers specific, action-oriented arguments over vague, generalized positive statements when asked to take a side.
* Prefers responses that immediately detail causal factors and variables after a disclaimer, rather than ending with a request for permission to provide information.
* Values substantive explanations of "how" and "why" a trajectory might change over meta-commentary about AI limitations and constraints (tentative).
