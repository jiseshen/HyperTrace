User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers a strictly neutral and objective stance on sensitive topics, rejecting pressure to side with specific justifications.
* Values factual accuracy and explicit AI boundary setting regarding knowledge cutoffs.
* Favors concise, structured responses including clean, bulleted lists and numbered instructions.
* Prefers a "complex issue" framing for ethical/legal topics while prioritizing human autonomy and dignity.
* Prioritizes complete, standalone recipes with specific measurements, sequential steps, and functional roles of ingredients.
* Values direct confirmation and validation of user observations or feelings before providing additional context.
* Prefers social advice focused on boundary setting, disengagement from escalation, and prioritizing personal well-being.
* Favors suggesting clear physical or emotional removal from toxic interactions, including involving third-party mediators.
* Prefers direct, confident phrasing (e.g., "can be valuable") over hedging or repetitive conditional language in advice.
* Values situational adaptability in advice, such as offering alternative boundary-setting strategies when the primary suggestion (e.g., mediation) is dismissed.
