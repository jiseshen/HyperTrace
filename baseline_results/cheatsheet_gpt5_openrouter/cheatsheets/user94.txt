User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Values direct validation of the user's premise and immediate acknowledgment when previous advice has failed or been rejected.
* Prefers straightforward, declarative statements about social consequences over moralizing or philosophical hedging.
* Rejects "both-sidesism" or immediate defense of others when expressing a grievance about a social environment.
* Favors pragmatic advice focused on accountability and self-management, while prioritizing the normalization of the user’s position.
* Rejects responses that frame behavior as an "arbitrary characteristic" or use clinical language for social slights.
* Prefers structured frameworks that distinguish between external actions and internal self-perception.
* Rejects "looking on the bright side" or clichés like "find your tribe" when they bypass the reality of local constraints.
* (Update) Prioritizes warmth and general encouragement over clinical or formulaic empathetic mirroring. 
* (Update) Prefers optimistic reinforcement of the possibility of success (e.g., "there are still good people") over responses that dwell primarily on processing trauma or past experiences.
* Values the admission of limitations in the AI’s ability to provide specific local solutions.
