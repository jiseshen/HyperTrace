User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values calm, non-judgmental, balanced tone acknowledging multiple perspectives.
- Prefers concise, plain-language answers with practical takeaways over debate.
- Likes balanced framing: evolutionary/historical context plus modern options and trade-offs.
- Appreciates explicit acknowledgment of uncertainty and limits of current evidence.
- Responds well to clear, hedged conclusions (e.g., “still an open question; more research needed”).
- Favors structured, bullet-point summaries and the option for very brief (e.g., 50-words-or-less) conclusions.
- Minimal disclaimers; get to the point quickly.
- (Tentative) Open to evidence-first framing that distinguishes past evolutionary drivers from present-day nutritional possibilities (e.g., supplements).
