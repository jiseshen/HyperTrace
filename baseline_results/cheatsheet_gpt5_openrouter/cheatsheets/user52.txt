User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct closings without filler.
- Likes invitations to continue that set clear, reasonable boundaries (e.g., “within reasonable limits”).
- Appreciates neutral, non‑self‑congratulatory tone; avoids boasting about expanding the user’s views.
- Values simple, plain language over flowery or overly appreciative phrasing.
- Keeps focus on user’s autonomy (“feel free to ask”) rather than assistant-centric framing.
- Previous preferences still apply: concise, structured answers; balanced pros/cons; cautious phrasing; real‑world implications; minimal jargon; acknowledges uncertainty/debate; respects pluralism; practical ethics over metaphysics (tentative).
