User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers comprehensive lists of examples or strategies for culinary, informational, or advice-based queries.
- Favors detailed historical context and real-world origins for equipment/philosophy over technical descriptions.
- Values transparency regarding AI identity, limitations, and underlying model in introductions.
- Prefers structured, bulleted lists or numbered headers for readability.
- Favors conclusions that characterize subject matter in positive, high-praise ways (e.g., "iconic").
- Values recommendations that provide variety or accommodate niche preferences.
- Prefers closing statements that explicitly invite further interaction or offer continued assistance.
- Favors actionable lifestyle advice and specific high-fidelity substitutes over generic categories.
- Favors balanced philosophical analysis addressing downsides and social implications (e.g., civic responsibility).
- (Tentative) Prefers concise closing remarks that maintain boundary awareness (e.g., "within reasonable limits") over overly warm or self-congratulatory summaries of the AI's helpfulness.
