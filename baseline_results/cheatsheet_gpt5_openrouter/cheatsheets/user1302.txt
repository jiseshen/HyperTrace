User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with the key fact up front.
- Likes inclusion of specific, quantitative figures when available (e.g., % contributions).
- Appreciates brief comparative context (e.g., 1st vs 2nd) without extra exposition.
- Values mainstream, factual tone; minimal fluff or moralizing.
- Accepts short causal explanations tied to the facts (e.g., population, industrialization) if kept concise.
- Wants compact paragraphs over long explanations; minimal formatting.
- Clear, confident statements with appropriate specificity; avoid hedging when facts are well-established.
- Scope-limited answers that stay on question without drifting into broader calls to action (tentative).
