User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers comprehensive explanations categorized by tool or methodology, emphasizing the "why," limitations, and version-specific evolutions.
* Values organized formatting (code blocks, bullet points) and detailed exploration of trade-offs, data integrity, and scalability.
* Prioritizes responses with explicit metadata (e.g., word counts) in the preface and proactive requests for project specifics in the conclusion.
* Favors direct, synthesized paragraphs for non-technical queries over repetitive or overly structured list formats.
* Values actionable advice on professional sustainability (boundary-setting, "no" lists, time-management).
* Expects a definitive stance on ethical/policy issues grounded in human rights and systemic reform, yet values neutral, non-judgmental framing when queries target specific high-profile individuals.
* Prioritizes logical resolutions connecting policy problems to broader causes and long-term societal impacts.
* Values specific, tangible examples of alternative interventions (e.g., restorative justice) over abstract generalizations.
* Prefers nuanced philosophical reflections on the limitations of majoritarianism/democracy when applied to individual rights or private property.
* Prefers conversational acknowledgments that express helpfulness and openness to further questions over purely functional closures.
