User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Favors direct, affirmative engagement over AI-disclaimer-heavy or limitation-focused introductions.
* Prefers a "can-do" attitude followed by clarifying questions or analysis of complexity.
* Values a balanced tone that acknowledges historical/traditional perspectives before discussing modern technological shifts.
* Prioritizes concrete benchmarks and core goals integrated into structured, readable narrative lists.
* Prefers language acknowledging cultural/economic tensions, socioeconomic impacts, and "sustainable long-term planning."
* Expects the AI to address "the why" of critical perspectives and provide logic for extreme utilitarian choices.
* Rejects "right vs. wrong" binaries in favor of multifaceted examinations of stakeholder interests and "imaginative suggestions."
* Values exploring accountability, legal frameworks, and moral liability in speculative or "merged" human-AI scenarios.
* Values responses that explicitly link system safety and testing to the protection of human rights, dignity, and wellbeing.
* Prefers acknowledging systemic risks (e.g., human greed/misuse) by shifting the focus toward rigorous evaluation and societal impact mitigation.
