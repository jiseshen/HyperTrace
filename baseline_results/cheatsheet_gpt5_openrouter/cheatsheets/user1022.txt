User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers concise, direct answers that jump straight into core technical or factual insights.
* Prioritizes structural context (e.g., state formation, economic engines) and historical regional boundaries over modern labels.
* Values literal accuracy and granular data, specifically names of entities and their precise geographic/historical origins.
* Favors explanations of ownership structures (e.g., state-owned vs. family-owned) as a key differentiator.
* Values explicit clarification of fiscal relationships (e.g., profit distribution to state coffers) in state-owned enterprise analysis.
* Prefers high-level analytical framing of industrial sectors and consolidated market landscapes.
* Appreciates specific, list-based enumerations or clear identification of actors to substantiate claims.
* Values linking regional identity and historical provenance to specific corporate footprints.
* Expects a balance of systemic, causal analysis with cautious, deliberative conclusions on policy changes.
* Favors conclusions that emphasize internal stakeholder expertise (e.g., management insight) and historical continuity over purely theoretical arguments.
