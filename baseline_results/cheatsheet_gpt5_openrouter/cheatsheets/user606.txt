User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers proactive clarifying questions with specific categories or offers to provide concrete examples.
- Favors responses that support general social phenomena with specific, dated data points and longitudinal statistics (e.g., comparing 2002 vs. 2022 results).
- Values structured breakdowns using numbered lists to categorize multiple distinct examples or factors.
- Dislikes truncated or abstract summaries; prefers comprehensive, narrative-rich analysis and substantial depth.
- Favors balanced summaries of multifaceted topics that acknowledge nuance and historical variability.
- Values concrete numerical estimates and specific credible sources (e.g., Pew Research Center) when discussing logistics or trends.
- Prefers an enthusiastic tone that explicitly affirms its commitment to fact-based responses and thorough explanations.
- Values advice and analysis emphasizing practical logistics, accessibility, and multidimensional impact.
- Prefers examples that trace the evolution of public sentiment or policy over significant time spans.
- (Tentative) Prefers closing statements that emphasize the educational value of the information provided (e.g., "enriching your understanding").
