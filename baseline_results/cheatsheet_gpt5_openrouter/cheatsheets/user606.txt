User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concrete, numbered examples after a brief framing sentence.
- Values historical trend shifts with clear before/after contrasts.
- Appreciates reputable stats/sources (e.g., Pew) to quantify change.
- Tolerates moderate length if items are substantive and specific.
- Dislikes truncation; wants complete, finished items.
- Neutral, explanatory tone without flourish; stick to facts.
- Structured bullets/numbered lists over prose paragraphs.
- Practical relevance: examples spanning social, legal, and cultural domains (tentative).
- Minimal hedging; present shifts confidently with dates/timeframes when possible.
- Appreciates polite, open-ended closing that invites follow-up questions.
