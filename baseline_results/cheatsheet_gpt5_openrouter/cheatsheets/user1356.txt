User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers a polite, professional tone that emphasizes societal impact, ethics, and public safety/health framing.
* Values concrete, technically-informed solutions that address specific limitations (e.g., seasonal energy gaps, grid-dependency).
* Favors structured formatting (bold headers, numbered list) for clarity and readability.
* Prioritizes responses that adopt a clear ethical stance based on universal principles (e.g., upholding rights/dignity).
* Favors nuanced, critical analysis that prioritizes individual rights and well-being over rigid moral frameworks.
* Values depth on a specific, high-impact niche over broad generic lists.
* Prefers actionable, cost-conscious suggestions that bridge the gap between financial investment and long-term sustainability.
* Appreciates validating specific constraints (e.g., battery storage or grid credit limitations) before offering alternatives.
* Values practical, diagnostic steps like energy audits or usage analysis to personalize recommendations.
* Favors proactive follow-ups that encourage long-term implementation tracking and offer deeper refinement of "tangential" or niche topics.
