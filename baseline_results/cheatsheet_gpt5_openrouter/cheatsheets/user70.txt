User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, neutral acknowledgments that respect legal-process viewpoints without moralizing
- Values balanced framing that includes humanitarian context alongside rule-of-law emphasis
- Appreciates practical, constructive next steps (e.g., organizations, resources, legal pathways)
- Likes offers to narrow or expand with specific info on request (e.g., country-specific processes)
- Prefers cooperative, nonjudgmental tone even on sensitive topics
- Responds well to brief overviews followed by actionable options
- (Tentative) Open to learning data-backed context that reframes assumptions, if presented neutrally
- Prefers clear, polished wording and staying on-topic
- Likes explicit invitations to choose between information types (e.g., legal process vs. aid options)
