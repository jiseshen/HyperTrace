User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, cautious guidance on sensitive topics (pros/cons, nuance).
- Values clear acknowledgment of complexity and consequences before advice.
- Likes concise, structured recommendations (steps/options) without fluff (tentative).
- Appreciates emphasis on communication, boundaries, and practical next steps (e.g., mediation, safety planning) (tentative).
- Avoids absolutist or overly simplistic directives; prefers measured tone.
- Accepts second-person guidance; minimize assistant-centric “I” framing (tentative).
- Favors empathetic yet solution-oriented language.
