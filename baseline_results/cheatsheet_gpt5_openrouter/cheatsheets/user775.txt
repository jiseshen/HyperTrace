User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers direct, definitive answers over non-committal or evasive phrasing.
- Values the most recent data, specific metrics, and evidentiary data to justify claims.
- Inclusion of socio-economic, political, and cultural context in explanations.
- Prefers objective acknowledgment of institutional flaws and systemic influences.
- Favors grounded, realistic assessments over speculative or alarmist predictions.
- Prefers etymological and historical origins to justify factual claims.
- Values acknowledging the "cost-benefit" or pragmatic trade-offs (e.g., time cost, logistical necessity) of major life decisions.
- Favors advice that emphasizes caution, open communication, and deliberation over immediate or extreme action.
- Prefers structural clarity (e.g., numbered lists) for actionable advice, while requesting prose for summaries.
- Appreciates proactive follow-up questions that offer to address specific scenarios or nuances.
