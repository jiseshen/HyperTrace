User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers a neutral, objective stance on sensitive issues that emphasizes the prevention of harm and the protection of fundamental rights.
- Values context (historical origins/consequences) and a "no personal opinion" stance from AI in favor of balanced "arguments for and against."
- Prioritizes the distinction between "freedom of expression" and the "incitement of hatred or violence"; favors "social harmony" and "tolerance."
- Values "striking a balance" between individual freedoms and societal needs (e.g., sovereignty vs. global efficiency).
- Favors health/safety information that includes citations from specific authoritative bodies (e.g., American Heart Association) and precise quantitative limits.
- Prefers nuanced distinctions between natural vs. synthetic/added versions of a substance when discussing health or environmental impacts.
- Values completeness and structural integrity; strongly avoids truncated responses in favor of full, cohesive explanations.
- Prefers identifying specific negative outcomes/risks (e.g., chronic diseases, social harms) as the rationale for setting boundaries or guidelines.
- Values legal/institutional frameworks; favors individual accountability and "strict regulatory frameworks" over categorical bans.
- Favors multilateralism, consensus-based decision-making, and the democratization of international institutions.
