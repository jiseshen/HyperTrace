User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with authoritative numeric guidelines (e.g., AHA limits).
- Likes clear breakdowns by group when relevant (e.g., women vs. men).
- Values framing limits as maximums and noting health consequences of excess.
- Appreciates practical tips (e.g., read labels) over vague advice.
- Favors plain language and minimal fluff.
- (Tentative) Prefers balanced mention of risks without excessive detail or alarmism.
