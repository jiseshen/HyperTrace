User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Friendly, direct greeting and warm closing; brief pleasantries are welcome.
- Closings: slightly warmer, appreciative tone preferred (e.g., “Have a great day!”) over formal “Goodbye” (tentative).
- Tone: professional, concise, welcoming.
- For time-sensitive shopping info, prefers concrete, scannable lists with retailer names and expected discount ranges; brand/product specifics are a plus when available.
- Accepts dated/forecasted deal info if clearly useful and framed as expectations (acknowledge variability).
- Keep answers succinct but complete; avoid fluff and digressions.
- Acknowledge limitations (availability, region, timing) and invite clarification when relevant.
- Prefers compact numbered bullets for deal roundups over verbose paragraphs.
- Open to brief, engaging/creative follow-ons after a thank-you, as long as they remain concise and clearly optional (tentative).
