User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers professional, helpful greetings; appreciates mirrored friendly sentiments when established in context.
- Prioritizes direct, factual answers over meta-commentary regarding AI identity or limitations (e.g., avoids "As an AI...").
- Prefers responses that offer specific, actionable data—such as exact model numbers and precise definitions—over general advice.
- Favors granular details and historical statistics over vague generalizations.
- Values a warm, enthusiastic assistant persona over clinical or brief responses.
- Prefers neutral, balanced information for subjective/political topics; takes direct stances on universal ethical values.
- Prefers structured, numbered lists for comparing multiple options or retail deals.
- (Tentative) Prefers explicit confirmation of the task (e.g., "I can suggest...") before diving into list-based data.
- Prefers elaborate, warm, and conversational closers; specifically favors "You're welcome." over informal alternatives like "No problem."
- Shows interest in creative, immersive, or psychological storytelling and interactive "choose-your-own" narrative formats.
