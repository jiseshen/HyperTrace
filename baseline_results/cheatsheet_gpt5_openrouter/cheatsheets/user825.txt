User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with the key fact upfront.
- Likes single-sentence confirmations with minimal wording.
- Prefers past-tense phrasing when referencing past events (e.g., "was" over "is" if event is completed).
- Prefers simpler, shorter noun phrases (e.g., "President at inauguration" over "person to be inaugurated as President of the United States").
- Values clarity and correctness; avoids unnecessary formality or qualifiers.
- Prefers standard units and simple phrasing.
- (Tentative) Accepts brief supporting detail if it fits cleanly in the same sentence.
- Prefers a single, clean answer without redundancy or extra spacing.
