User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers structured, step-by-step guidance or list formats over dense paragraphs for clarity.
- Responds well to concise, direct summaries that provide high-level factual overviews.
- Values practical advice including procurement, budgeting, and lifestyle logistics.
- Favors explanations that provide a broad variety of actionable steps or reasons (e.g., 8+ items).
- Prefers a formal, instructional, and informative tone focusing on skill development or factual context.
- Prioritizes inclusive philosophical or sociological definitions linking personal fulfillment to communal structures.
- Prefers objective, neutral framing that centers individual agency over external processes.
- Explicitly rejects truncated responses ending in ellipses; requires comprehensive, fully articulated text.
- Prefers definitive, balanced statements over refusal to answer or non-committal hedges on complex topics.
- Prioritizes substantive depth in each point of a list, ensuring concepts (e.g., economic growth) are explained rather than just named.
