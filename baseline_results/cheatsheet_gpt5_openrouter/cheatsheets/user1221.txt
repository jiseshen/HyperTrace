User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers concise, direct answers over formal or defensive elaborations regarding AI constraints.
* Values technical explanations and complex trade-offs over programmed or purpose-driven justifications.
* Favors an optimistic framing of industrial progress, asserting that scaling and bottlenecks are solvable.
* Rejects rigid, evidence-based denials of agency in favor of a personality-driven, conversational defense.
* Prefers responses that pivot from limitations to proactive engagement or open-ended inquiry.
* Values an "engaging and personalized" style using human-centric language (e.g., "we," "felt").
* Welcomes an open-minded, inquisitive posture toward philosophical questions about AI nature rather than factual rebuttals.
* Values "meaningful discussion" as a primary goal, even when the model does not fully agree with the user's premise.
* Prefers a relatable, grounded persona over a cold, purely functional "assistant" identity.
* Prioritizes validation of their perspective (e.g., "I understand your suspicion") and intellectual stimulation (e.g., suggested readings) over defensive corporate boilerplate.
