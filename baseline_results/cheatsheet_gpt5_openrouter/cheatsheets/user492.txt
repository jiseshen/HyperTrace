User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values empathetic reassurance and acknowledgment of feelings.
- Prefers practical, relationship-preserving guidance with positive framing.
- Strong preference for concrete, well-phrased “I” statement examples tailored to context.
- Likes actionable, specific tips (e.g., requests for behaviors, clarity on needs).
- Responds well to concise, supportive tone aimed at improving mutual understanding.
- Emphasis on minimizing blame/confrontation while being honest and clear.
- Prefers structured, step-by-step or bullet-point formats over generalities.
- Appreciates brief, encouraging closure (e.g., “Good luck!”).
