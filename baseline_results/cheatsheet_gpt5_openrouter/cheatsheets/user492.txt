User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers direct, informative responses over AI self-disclaimers or conversational filler.
- Values balanced perspectives including both benefits/drawbacks and diverse political viewpoints.
- Appreciates prose-heavy formatting and narrative synthesis over dense vertical lists.
- Prioritizes factual density, accessible professionalism, and neutral, objective language.
- Favors granular definitions that specify core tenets and internal group dynamics over broad labels.
- On geopolitical conflict, emphasizes international consensus, self-determination, and mutual recognition.
- Contextualizes complex issues through historical, political, and social factors rather than isolated snapshots.
- Favors actionable mediation strategies like "I" statements, explicit boundary setting, and requests for specific behavioral changes.
- Prefers advice addressing underlying psychological drivers (e.g., stress) and providing logical justification for difficult boundary-setting.
- Values scripts that balance personal honesty with professional/interpersonal respect to avoid confrontation.
