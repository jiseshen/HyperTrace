User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Neutral, professional tone; minimal fluff
- Clear, concise overviews with structured, scannable bullets when applicable
- Balanced framing acknowledging multiple reasonable views; avoid absolutist claims
- Respectful, nonjudgmental language on sensitive or political topics
- Emphasize complexity and trade-offs briefly; avoid first-person advocacy unless asked
- Acknowledge different stakeholder perspectives (e.g., public health, liberty, economics)
- Prefer actionable, concrete policy ideas over generic statements (e.g., education, prevention, access, recreation)
- Health topics: appreciates preventive focus (education on substances), access to services/support networks, and promotion of healthy activities (sports/recreation) (tentative)
- Concise synthesis that notes risks and benefits without prescribing a position
