User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with a clear primary claim.
- Values brief, balanced nuance acknowledging variability by goals.
- Likes high-level framing followed by a clear, compact list of key items.
- Neutral, objective tone; minimal fluff.
- Accepts general health/fitness guidance emphasizing completeness/coverage.
- (Tentative) Prefers holistic recommendations over narrow or overly specific lists.
- Prefers inclusion of common anatomical terms with parenthetical specific names.
- Appreciates mention of primary targets first, then secondary/stabilizer muscles in a single succinct paragraph.
- Prefers metric units (kg-based dosing) over imperial when giving intake ranges.
- Likes inclusion of a brief professional-consultation caveat for personalization.
