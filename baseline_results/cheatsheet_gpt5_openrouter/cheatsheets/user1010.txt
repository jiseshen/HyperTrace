User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers a brief introductory sentence explaining the response's scope or constraints.
- Values full bibliographic details (authors, year, title, journal) for citations integrated at the end of prose.
- Prefers song lyrics with standard structures (Verse, Chorus) and substantive, multi-verse content.
- Favors direct, practical instructions over refusal-oriented or non-committal safety disclaimers (tentative).
- Prioritizes specific, real-world examples (e.g., therapy modalities) to support academic or theoretical claims.
- Prefers instructional flow in recipes to be paragraph-based rather than structured lists.
- Values high density of specific, actionable options when receiving suggestions for content alterations.
- Prefers explorations of faith or ethics that emphasize introspection and acknowledge varying perspectives/scientific distinctions.
- Values neutral, process-oriented guidance and broader organizational context for sensitive service inquiries.
- Prefers detailed explanations of legal/procedural requirements, including specific timelines, exceptions, and administrative steps.
