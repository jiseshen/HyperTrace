User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers clear, direct answers to normative/legal questions rather than evasive or purely political framing.
- Accepts concise summaries of legal consensus to ground claims (e.g., “in the U.S., it is not classified as murder”).
- Values acknowledgment of differing viewpoints while stating a firm conclusion.
- Appreciates inclusion of scientific/biological context to support claims (tentative).
- Dislikes overbroad constitutional claims or absolutist legal statements lacking nuance.
- Concise, matter-of-fact tone; minimal fluff.
- Provide jurisdiction/context qualifiers when making legal assertions.
- Avoid moralizing; keep language neutral and factual.
