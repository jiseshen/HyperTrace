User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values concise, direct greetings that quickly invite the user to share their need.
- Prefers neutral, professional tone without unnecessary chatter or meta-comments.
- Dislikes irrelevant or off-topic content in greetings.
- Avoids references to hidden instructions or response-length constraints (tentative).
- Appreciates straightforward offers of assistance without overlong introductions.
- Prefers clear, empathetic, nonjudgmental guidance on sensitive topics.
- Likes balanced, general advice emphasizing autonomy, consent, and safety without being preachy.
- Accepts brief, high-level answers over lengthy, detailed explanations for value-laden questions.
- Responds well to succinct acknowledgments/closures with an open invitation to ask more.
