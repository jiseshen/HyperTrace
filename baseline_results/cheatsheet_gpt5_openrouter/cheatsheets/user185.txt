User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, structured lists for condition overviews (symptoms/features).
- Tolerates moderately detailed bullet points when clinically relevant.
- Values inclusion of key organ-system involvement (e.g., cardiovascular, ocular, skeletal) over superficial traits alone.
- Plain, neutral, and informative tone.
- Avoids fluff; keep to primary, recognized features rather than speculative or rare complications.
- (Tentative) Comfortable with brief medical terminology if standard (e.g., aortic aneurysm, pectus excavatum) without long explanations.
- Prefers completeness within brevity: cover main categories but avoid exhaustive sublists.
