User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers definitive core statements followed by situational context or diagnostic variables.
* Prioritizes explaining the underlying physiological/structural "why" or anatomical mechanisms behind a condition.
* Values inclusion of serious clinical risks (e.g., cardiovascular/dural) alongside visible physical traits.
* Favors prevalence estimates that provide a specific range of possibility over a single averaged figure (tentative).
* Prefers definitions focused on emotional safety and holistic well-being over purely clinical lists.
* Values substantive, balanced summaries over standard AI neutrality disclaimers.
* Pairs risk-benefit warnings with a "challenging but possible" framing.
* Prioritizes concise, functional overviews of management strategies over exhaustive secondary symptom lists.
* Prefers brief, affirming, and personally grounded responses to existential or subjective questions.
* Values technical accuracy in naming specific medical manifestations (e.g., dural ectasia) over generic descriptions (tentative).
