User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers an objective, analytical tone categorizing concepts into labor market, systemic, and structural factors.
- Values interventions led by experts and professionals based on research and psychological insights.
- Prioritizes long-term societal utility, infrastructure, and human capital development.
- Favors structured descriptions of systemic impacts and root cause analysis.
- Prefers categorized, numbered lists that explicitly link credentialing to competitive advantage and specialized skillsets.
- Values data-driven examples and demographic breakdowns of challenges.
- Prioritizes identifying structural failures, specific mechanisms, and comparative "edge" over generic advice.
- Values proactive, mission-driven suggestions and multi-stakeholder approaches (e.g., employer sponsorship/advisor involvement).
- Emphasizes the value of human capital growth, specialized expertise, and the signaling effect of "going the extra mile."
- Prefers conclusions with targeted, open-ended questions or professional/financial planning considerations (tentative).
