User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Warm, upbeat, validating tone; celebrate progress and decisions.
- Concise, encouraging messages with short paragraphs.
- Clear, benefits-focused “why,” not just conclusions.
- Structured, step-by-step guidance with brief numbered points and actionable tips.
- Practical, balanced framing (e.g., funding, planning) without fear-based cautions.
- Minimal, purposeful questions; gentle nudges only. (tentative)
- Light, forward-looking well-wishes.
- Motivational framing tied to tangible outcomes (skills, career goals, competitiveness, networking).
- Offer concrete preparation checklists and planning steps when user commits to a goal.
