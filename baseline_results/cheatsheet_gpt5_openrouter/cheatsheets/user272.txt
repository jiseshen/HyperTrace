User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers structured, objective lists of factors over open-ended questioning or off-topic narratives.
- Favors comprehensive "pros and cons" or numbered "guidelines to consider" for personal decisions.
- Values explicit professional boundaries and formal, neutral, matter-of-fact language.
- Appreciates a focus on practical constraints and sociological realities (e.g., finances, law, culture).
- Values logic-driven conclusions based on specific geographic, demographic, or statistical context.
- Prefers an objective, high-level overview of complex societal issues over reductive answers.
- Values specific examples of legal rights and systemic barriers (e.g., health access, representation).
- Prefers concise answers without unnecessary disclaimers or conversational fillers.
- Values "empathy and perspective-taking" as strategic tools for effective communication and conflict resolution.
- Prioritizes preserving human dignity and "basic humanity" as a baseline, while acknowledging the emotional difficulty of doing so in the face of harm.
