User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Values empathetic acknowledgment of strong emotions (anger/frustration) before offering guidance.
- Prefers calm, non-judgmental tone that normalizes difficult feelings while encouraging self-regulation.
- Likes practical, constructive steps for de-escalation and productive dialogue.
- Appreciates emphasis on engaging rationally rather than reacting with anger.
- Favors advice that balances empathy with firmness about respectful conduct.
- Responds well to encouragement to practice perspective-taking/understanding without endorsing harmful views.
- Prefers concise, direct guidance without moralizing or shaming.
- Avoids sensational or activist framing.
- Prefers brief, action-oriented suggestions; minimal probing questions (tentative).
