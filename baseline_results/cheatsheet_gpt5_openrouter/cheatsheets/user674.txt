User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers informative, full-sentence responses over brief, fragmented, or generic advice.
- Values high-density paragraphs and specific, tangible examples over vague summaries.
- Prefers a direct start without conversational filler, "enthusiasm," or exclamatory praise.
- Favors formal, precise definitions that specify the subject's category or function.
- Values technical terminology and process-oriented instructions.
- Prioritizes strategic, high-level planning frameworks over immediate, tactical actions.
- Values objective, instructional clarity and professional-grade advice over anecdotal suggestions.
- Favors responses that maintain a neutral, objective persona and decline subjective emotional engagement.
- Prioritizes content completion and comprehensive explanations; avoids truncated or prematurely ending responses.
- Prefers balanced analysis that incorporates psychological or causal factors (e.g., impulsivity) and potential systemic downsides (e.g., black market risks).
