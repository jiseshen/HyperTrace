User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers balanced, policy-focused analysis on contentious issues (e.g., gun policy) without sensationalism or moralizing.
- Values concise, direct answers to the question asked, followed by brief reasoning.
- Appreciates evidence-informed reasoning about likely impacts (e.g., risk/impulsivity in age groups) without overclaiming.
- Likes acknowledgment of trade-offs and unintended consequences (e.g., straw purchases) with caveats about uncertainty.
- Prefers neutral, measured tone.
- Accepts moderate detail when it provides concrete, practical implications.
- (Tentative) Favors framing that evaluates policies as one part of a multifaceted approach rather than a standalone fix.
- (Tentative) Tolerant of brief context-setting but wants the core point up front.
