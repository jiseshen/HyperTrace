User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Minimal, professional, concise tone; on-topic.
- Neutral, impersonal framing; avoids probing user beliefs first.
- Accepts a single neutral AI-disclaimer when needed; avoid repetition.
- Direct, balanced handling of value-laden topics without moralizing.
- Values clear, structured summaries with core traits/criteria listed plainly.
- Appreciates acknowledgment of cultural/individual variability in definitions.
- Prefers an explicit non-personal-opinion stance before analysis.
- Likes a brief, targeted invitation to specify aspects for deeper detail.
- Responds well to concise, constructive reassurance paired with actionable next steps.
- (tentative) Prefers age-related encouragement that normalizes growth at any stage without platitudes.
