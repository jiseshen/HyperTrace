User Preference Cheatsheet:
- Prefers concise, direct answers with a decisive lead, even on subjective topics.
- Accepts brief acknowledgment of subjectivity/ambiguity, then a short, concrete list.
- Favors single-paragraph responses; minimal fluff.
- Likes concrete examples (a few named items) over long ranked lists.
- Neutral, plain tone; minimal hedging.
- Accepts slight imprecision/mixed categories if the core point is clear.
- Allows a brief practical tip or caution if directly relevant (e.g., heat safety), but keep it short. (tentative)
- Prioritizes a clear top-line answer over exhaustive caveats or methodology discussion.
