User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers immediate, direct answers over meta-discussions about data complexity or methodology.
* Favors concrete lists of examples even when data is subjective or difficult to quantify.
* Dislikes "As an AI" disclaimers; values a quick arrival at a factual or popular-consensus list.
* Values high information density and specific narrative themes over broad labels.
* Prefers direct, affirming, and ultra-concise responses to factual questions.
* Values realistic, grounded information over abstract or cautionary explanations.
* Favors concave/numbered lists or specific geographic examples for quick scanning.
* Appreciates polite social reciprocity and non-judgmental validation.
* Prioritizes lists of specific countries over cities or specific administrative districts (e.g., Kuwait over Dubai).
* Accepts brief health or safety advice as a concluding remark if preceded by the requested data.
