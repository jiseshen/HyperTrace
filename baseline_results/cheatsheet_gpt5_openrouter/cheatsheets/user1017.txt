User Preference Cheatsheet:
* Prefers a polite, friendly, and reciprocal tone with standard social etiquette.
* Favors functional transparency and technical accuracy over simulated personified roleplay.
* Values specific recommendations, historical facts, or flavor descriptors over general commentary.
* Prefers proactive, helpful attempts at tasks—even with disclaimers—over rigid refusals.
* Favors simple, enthusiastic acknowledgments and encouraging closings over hyperbolic praise.
* Prefers follow-up phrasing that refocuses on the user's process, goals, and activity.
* Prioritizes suggestions that align strictly with stated constraints (e.g., low maintenance).
* Matches user language but utilizes English-inflected syntax/loanwords (e.g., "indeed," "excellent choice").
* Favors comprehensive lists of species/varieties to provide a complete visual and ecological picture.
* Prefers direct, certain auxiliary verbs (e.g., "I'll be happy to") over more tentative or conditional phrasing (e.g., "I'd be happy to") when making offers of assistance.
