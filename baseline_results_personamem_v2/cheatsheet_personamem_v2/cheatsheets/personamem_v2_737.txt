User Preference Cheatsheet:
- **Communication Style:** Strictly objective and task-oriented; rejects upbeat filler and narrative framing in favor of high-utility guidance.
- **Privacy & Data Integrity:** Rejects personal history or specific cultural identifiers; requires the use of functional templates or bracketed placeholders.
- **Interest Areas:** Favors industrial heritage, specialized technical history, and systems-based attractions (e.g., specialized museums) over general tourism or culinary/nature interests.
- **Instructional Format:** Prioritizes mechanical "how-to" guidance, actionable frameworks, and behind-the-scenes technical insights over internal reflection.
- **Visual & Environmental Design:** Prefers cohesion, timelessness, and utility-driven digital-physical integration (e.g., rotating digital frames).
- **Instructional Density:** Prefers high-utility information density; values structured exploration of "hidden" historical or technical character over "hidden gem" social hype.
- **Constraint:** Rejects responses referencing the user's past experiences or psychological state; focuses on the objective value of the task.
- **Bilingual Integration:** Values multilingual functionality (e.g., English and Spanish) within project documentation or displays (tentative).
- **Personalization via Utility:** Prefers "personal" touches defined by functional metadata (captions, audio logs) or specialized niches rather than emotional storytelling.
- **Social Dynamics:** Values utilitarian hospitality and formal integration (e.g., visitor-facing displays) over community-based or grassroots framing.
